How it works

From raw idea to reel in four steps

You type one line in Claude Code. The skills do the rest, and the scoring tools run on your own computer.

  1. Tell it the idea

    One sentence is enough. If the idea is thin, it asks you one question instead of padding it.

    /ig-reel the wrist mistake that ruins your perfume

  2. Get three hooks, scored

    Three different formulas from the 26, each with the spoken line and the on-screen line. The scorer ranks them; if the best one is under 50, you don't have a hook yet.

  3. Shoot from a timed script

    Script, on-screen cards of six words or fewer, and a beat sheet that flags any shot that runs too long.

  4. Post it sounding human

    The humanizer strips the AI tells, and the caption skill writes the first line that survives the '... more' cut.

It flags what you'd only notice after posting: a hook with nothing checkable and beats with nothing concrete. You fix it before you shoot.

FAQ

Before you message us

What do I need to use it?

A Claude account with Claude Code (Pro plan or higher) and Python 3 on your computer for the scoring tools. Claude runs them for you; you don't write any code.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. You talk to Claude in Spanish or English and it writes your scripts, captions and DMs in either. The skills' internal instructions are in English and the automatic scorers were tuned on English hooks, so their numbers are most reliable in English.

Does it post or like things for me?

No. It never posts, likes or follows. It writes; you shoot and publish. That keeps your account clear of automation blocks.

How do I get it?

Once your payment is confirmed, we send you the pack with step-by-step install instructions. Installing it means copying one folder into your project and opening Claude Code.

Will it work for my niche?

Yes. Nothing in it is about perfume. It works from your voice profile and from what's working in your niche right now.

Where do the skills come from?

The 13 skills come from an open-source project (MIT license, © 2026 Jake Schincariol), and you receive them with that license included. What you pay for is the pack ready to use and help getting it installed.
